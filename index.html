About Me

Hey, I'm Liudmyla.

I'm a dental technician passionate about precision, anatomy and the small details that make a smile feel natural. My work blends digital design with handcraft — from Exocad modelling to wax-up and finely tuned orthodontic appliances.

I love what I do, and I'm always learning. Innovation, professional development and mentorship are a real passion of mine, just like the full anatomy of teeth. If you'd like to collaborate, learn together, or just connect — you're very welcome.
